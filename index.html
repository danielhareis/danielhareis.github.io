Daniel Reis

I am an economist and consultant working on competition policy, regulation, and infrastructure economics. My research uses empirical industrial organization to study how firms compete and how public policy shapes market outcomes.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of São Paulo (FEA-USP), with a visiting research period at the University of Oldenburg, Germany, and an MBA in Data Science and Analytics from USP. My professional experience spans economic consulting and Brazil’s competition authority (CADE), alongside consulting work for the Inter-American Development Bank and ANATEL.
