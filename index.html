Daniel Reis

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Economics, Business and Accounting of the University of São Paulo (FEA-USP). I work in empirical industrial organization, competition policy, and regulation. My research studies how firms compete and how public policy shapes market outcomes, with a focus on cartels, mergers, and market power in Brazilian industries such as cement, ready-mix concrete, and air transportation.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from FEA-USP, with a visiting research period at the University of Oldenburg, Germany. Before and alongside my academic work, I served at Brazil's competition authority (CADE), in both the Department of Economic Studies and the General Superintendence. I have also advised public institutions such as the Inter-American Development Bank, the Global Development Network, ANATEL, and the São Paulo State Secretariat of Infrastructure and Logistics. In addition, I work as an economic consultant on antitrust and regulatory matters.
